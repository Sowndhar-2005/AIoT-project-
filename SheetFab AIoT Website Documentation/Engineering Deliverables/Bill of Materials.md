## 🎯 Purpose & Scope
The Bill of Materials provides an exhaustive, 14 line-item structural, mechanical, and electrical component breakdown required to build the complete industrial control panel. It specifies part numbers, component descriptions, material specifications, quantities, and supplier references.

## 🔍 Engineering Verification & Quality Safeguards
- **Structural Integrity**: Specifies 1.5 mm CRCA steel for exterior shell and heavier 2.0 mm galvanized sheet for internal backplate to resist deflection under transformer/MCCB weight.
- **Electrical Standards**: Complies with IEC 60947-2 (MCCB/MCB) and IEC 61439-1 (low-voltage switchgear assemblies).
- **Download Action**: Fully integrated in the Document Centre with direct browser tab viewing and file download capabilities.
