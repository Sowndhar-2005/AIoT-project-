## 🎯 Purpose & Scope
The Certificate of Conformance formalizes the quality audit record for the manufactured electrical control enclosure batch. It certifies compliance with drawing dimensions, material standards, coating thickness specifications, ingress protection, and electrical earth continuity.

---

## ⚠️ Engineering Honesty & Release State

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Provisional Audit Record / Inspection Pending:**
> Within the SheetFab AIoT application and document center, this document is statused as **Provisional / Inspection Pending (Awaiting Metrology Approval)**.
> To prevent false quality assurance claims, the application does not invent fabricated inspector signatures, false employee identity numbers, or unverified physical approval timestamps until certified hardware testing is completed.

---

## 📑 Certified Inspection Standards & Scope

| Inspection Characteristic | Governing Standard | Specified Tolerance / Requirement | Documented Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dimensional Geometry** | ISO 2768-m | Medium Class Tolerance ($\pm 0.80 \text{ mm}$ for $500 \times 600 \text{ mm}$) | Verified via Metrology Model |
| **Raw Material Grade** | IS 513:2008 / ISO 3574 | CRCA Grade D, Deep Drawing Quality, $1.50 \pm 0.05 \text{ mm}$ | Certified Mill Test Certificate |
| **Dry Film Coating (DFT)** | ISO 2808 / ASTM D7091 | Pure Polyester RAL 7035, $60\ \mu\text{m} - 80\ \mu\text{m}$ | Specified (Pre-shipment verification) |
| **Ingress Protection** | IEC 60529 | IP66 (Dust-tight, protected against heavy seas/jets) | Design Conforming (PU foam gasket) |
| **Earth Bonding Continuity** | IEC 61439-1 | Protective bonding resistance $< 0.1\ \Omega$ from door to chassis | Inspection Checkpoint |
